title: Droost — intake
purpose: Plan a whole site from a design source with the human before anything is built. Loads the intake skill.
---

Load the **`intake`** skill and follow it. In short: open the intake with
the human's words and the source, serve the source and audit it, write the
content model and put it to droost, ask the human what the source cannot
say, write the roadmap, ask the human "Approve this roadmap?", and approve
it on their yes. No run opens until then.

This file is Claude Code's slash-command way of invoking that skill and holds
nothing of its own: every host reads the skill, so the procedure is the same
wherever the intake is driven from.
